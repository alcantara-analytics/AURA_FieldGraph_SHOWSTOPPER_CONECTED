# Product Story

Traditional systems store a final value.

AURA FieldGraph stores the value **and why it is believed**.

Each asset is built from observations. Each observation becomes evidence. Each fact keeps provenance, source quality and confidence. Independent evidence can reinforce a fact; contradictory evidence creates a conflict instead of silently overwriting the record.

The result is a local Customer 360 that can answer not only **what equipment exists**, but also **how certain we are, what evidence supports it, what changed, and what action follows**.
